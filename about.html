Who I am

I hold a master's degree in Electrical Power Engineering and have hands-on experience with PLC programming. Alongside that, I enjoy turning ideas into interfaces people can actually use — I like the moment a page goes from a wireframe to something that feels alive in the browser.

My day-to-day work sits on both sides of a project: JavaScript for the frontend, and Django for the backend. Outside of that, I spend time on personal projects, reading about design systems, and contributing small fixes to open-source repositories.

What drives me

I care about writing code that's easy to read a year later, and about interfaces that don't make people think harder than they need to. Coming from a power engineering background, I'm used to thinking in systems — how one part affects the whole — and I bring that same mindset to software architecture.

I'm especially interested in the overlap between reliable engineering and thoughtful design, whether that's a control panel built with a PLC or a web app built with Django.
