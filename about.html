about me

The short version of my story.

Who I am

I'm Saeid Hasan Abadi, an Electrical Engineer and programmer who enjoys turning ideas into interfaces people can actually use. I like the moment a page goes from a wireframe to something that feels alive in the browser.

Outside of coursework, I spend time on personal projects, reading about design systems, and contributing small fixes to open-source repositories.

What drives me

I care about writing code that's easy to read a year later, and about interfaces that don't make people think harder than they need to. I'm especially interested in the overlap between clean engineering and thoughtful design.
